Partly. The encoder is a pretrained VGG network used for feature extraction. AdaIN itself has no learned weights, and the decoder is custom-trained to reconstruct images from the stylized features.

The content and style images pass through the pretrained VGG encoder. AdaIN then aligns the mean and variance of the content features to those of the style features. The custom-trained decoder reconstructs an image from the result. PyTorch handles training and inference, and Flask serves the web interface.

It controls how strongly the style features replace the content features. At 0.00 the output stays close to the content image, and at 1.00 the stylization is applied at full strength. Values in between blend the two.

Painting-style images with strong texture and colour, such as impressionist, abstract, cubist, watercolour, or sketch works, tend to produce the most visible effect.
